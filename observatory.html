BCCS Evidence Observatory · Demonstrator 001

A working evidence record, open to inspection

The Observatory is in service at observatory.bccs.bio (opens observatory.bccs.bio). It holds one real measurement, with every input, the rule that was fixed beforehand, and the reason the method declined to give a figure. This page says what you will find there. The figures below are quoted from its public record as published on 20 November 2026.

The record’s own statement

BCCS ran a pre-registered measurement of the 2020 mangrove canopy extent of a 1,937 ha research area in Cần Giờ from 24 Sentinel-2 scenes (16 admitted) and 19 Sentinel-1 acquisitions, with every input frozen and every step replayable offline. The result was not robust to the pre-registered threshold brackets, so the method returned INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE and no extent is claimed.

How the method arrived at a refusal

Six steps, fixed in method d001-c01-0.1 before the data was opened. The first four were met. The fifth was not, so the sixth never produced a figure.

The same decision, five ways

The thresholds were registered with a bracket around each. The decision was repeated with each threshold at the end of its bracket.

  • Primary thresholdsSUPPORTED
  • Optical threshold at the low endSUPPORTED
  • Optical threshold at the high endCONFLICTING
  • Radar threshold at the low endSUPPORTED
  • Radar threshold at the high endCONFLICTING

Three give “supported”, two give “conflicting”. The rule asks for the same decision in all 5.

Why this is the useful result

With the primary thresholds alone the method would have reported “supported” and given an interval. The pre-registered robustness step is what stopped it.

A reader of the record can see that a different, defensible choice of threshold changes the answer. That is information a single figure would have hidden.

Two further sensitivity runs, registered in advance, gave the same outcome: the AOI mask of each grid eroded by one pixel; and the quarter of acquisitions with the highest predicted tide removed from each group.

Independent review: not reviewed

An acceptance report written by the stage that produced the result, and reproduction on the programme's own CI, are not independent scientific validation. The programme’s gate “Independent validation” is not started. How to review this work

What the record does not claim

Listed in the record itself, word for word:

  • Any change over time, trend, gain or loss
  • Any lifecycle state other than 01 Unverified
  • Canopy cover percentage inside mangrove stands, canopy height, biomass, carbon, biodiversity or ecosystem health
  • The condition of the site at any time after 30 April 2020
  • An error-adjusted area estimate (no probability reference sample exists)
  • An ecosystem-condition assessment for 2026 or any time after 30 April 2020
  • A carbon-stock or carbon-credit estimate
  • Biodiversity or ecosystem-health verification
  • A mangrove gain or loss between years
  • Independent field validation
  • A completed production PoPS verification
  • A lifecycle transition or a Base attestation
  • Species identification by radar: G-SAR detects canopy volume scattering, not mangroves

What is held back, and why

The method refused to state an extent. Under the programme’s claims discipline, nothing that would amount to an extent is published for a refused result.

  • Group extent fractions, pixel counts and variant intervals. The method refused to state an extent. Under the programme's claims discipline no extent figure or range for the site is published for a refused result, including the values of the individual evidence groups. The decisions, thresholds and data-quality figures are shown in full.
  • Agreement table between the groups. It is made of group pixel counts.
  • Statement recorded in the result record. It quotes the group values. The wording approved for external use is shown instead.
  • 6 map layers of the evidence groups and the reference. A class map of an evidence group shows that group's extent. The method refused to state an extent, and the programme does not publish group-level extent values for a refused result. Authorised reviewers can inspect the map in the operator workspace. The layers: Optical group: canopy class at the primary threshold; Optical group: valid observations per pixel; Radar group: canopy class at the primary threshold; Radar group: median VH backscatter; Radar group: valid observations per pixel; Reference: Global Mangrove Watch v3.0, 2020.
  • The canonical result record file. It carries the group values. Its SHA-256 is shown so that a reviewer given access can confirm they hold the same bytes.

The SHA-256 of each withheld file is public, so a reviewer who is given access can confirm they hold the same bytes.

What happens to this claim next

The next step is an open decision in the programme, published with the record:

  • A. Accept INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE as the C-01 result for the 2020 epoch and keep it unchanged.
  • B. Commission an independent calibration of the MVI and VH thresholds for Can Gio (data not used in this result), pre-register a method d001-c01-0.2 from it, and evaluate it as a new, separately versioned measurement.
  • C. Treat the bracket widths themselves as the open question for external review (Gate 7) before any new version.

Until that decision is taken and recorded, the outcome stands as it is. No threshold is adjusted after the fact, and no new method version is evaluated on data that was already seen.
