Request a proposal

Tell us the site, the claim and the decision

A few sentences are enough. You receive a written scope with a deliverable, a date and a price within two working days. If the question is one that open satellite evidence cannot answer, the reply says so.

For example: where the area is and roughly how large, what is being claimed about it and for which period, who has to decide what on the strength of it.
Add details if you have them (all optional)

This puts your answers into an email addressed to hq@kryonis.global, which you send from your own mail. Nothing is transmitted by this page. How it is handled: privacy.

Or write directly

hq@kryonis.global

KRYONIS Sovereign Systems Limited, Hong Kong SAR. Correspondence is in writing; a call can follow once the question is clear.

What happens next

  1. You receive a written reply within two working days.
  2. If the question fits, the reply is a one-page scope: deliverable, date, price.
  3. If it does not fit, the reply says why, and where you might turn instead.
  4. Nothing is charged and nothing starts until you confirm the scope in writing.

If no reply arrives in that time, something failed on our side. Please write again to the address above.
