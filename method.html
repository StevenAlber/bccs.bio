Method and limits

How a record is made, and what it cannot tell you

The value of a record lies in what it lets a stranger check. This page describes the mechanism and then spends as long on its limits.

Four things that are kept apart

Most confusion about “verified” data comes from folding these into one word. In a BCCS record each has its own field and its own value.

AxisThe question it answersDemonstrator 001 today
Processing stateDid the computation run to its end?SUCCEEDED
Evidence outcomeWhat does the evidence support under the rule fixed beforehand?INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
ReplayDoes the computation give the same result when run again, and who ran it?5 passed, 1 not run, 0 independent
Independent reviewHas someone outside the programme examined the method and the result?NOT REVIEWED

A run that succeeds can still end in a refusal, and a result that replays perfectly can still be wrong. See all four on the live record (opens observatory.bccs.bio)

Five words that are not synonyms

A hash match is not a replay. A replay is not validation. Validation by the author is not independent review. None of them is certification.

WordWhat it establishesWhat it does notBCCS today

CertificationAn accredited body attests conformity with a named standard.Anything outside that standard’s scope.None, and none is offered. BCCS is not a verification body.

The mechanism

The rule is registered before the result exists

The claim, the area boundary, the period, the thresholds with a bracket around each, the tolerances and the decision rule are written into one method file. Its SHA-256 is recorded before any measurement is computed. That is what shows the rule was not adjusted once the answer was known.

Every request leaves a receipt

Each call to a data source is recorded when it is made: what was asked, when, what came back, its size and its digest. Demonstrator 001 holds 296 of them. The inputs are then frozen.

The computation replays offline

The measurement runs from the stored bytes inside an environment with no network, so it cannot quietly fetch something newer. Run again from the same bytes, it must give the same record.

Two sensor groups and an external reference

Optical evidence (Sentinel-2) and radar evidence (Sentinel-1) are computed separately and compared with each other and with a published reference map, Global Mangrove Watch. Agreement has to hold within tolerances that were fixed beforehand.

The reference is the version named in the method file: for Demonstrator 001, Global Mangrove Watch version 3.0. A different version would be a new method version with its own registration, not an edit of this one.

Three outcomes, one of which is no

  • SUPPORTED with an interval, when the groups and the reference agree and the decision survives every bracket.
  • CONFLICTING when the evidence groups disagree beyond the tolerance.
  • INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE when coverage is too thin, or when the answer depends on where inside its bracket a threshold sits.

Refusing to conclude is a designed result, not a failure.

Publication is a separate decision

Nothing in the registry is public until a named person approves a publication set. For a refused result the programme publishes no extent figure and no map from which one could be read.

How independent the evidence is

From the programme record, word for word:

  • Optical imagery, radar and the reference map are all satellite remote sensing. They are one tier of evidence, not three independent lines.
  • All three use the same area polygon and pixel rule. The optical and radar groups also share the evaluation grid, the epoch and the programme's processing code, so a shared error would move them together.
  • Tidal state is the main correlated failure mode. It is addressed by compositing over many dates and by a tide sensitivity.
  • The reference is a published map, not ground truth. Part of its lineage is optical.
  • Radar detects any woody canopy and does not identify species. Mangrove attribution rests on the optical group and the reference.

What satellite evidence of this kind cannot do

See what is under the canopy

Pixels of 10 to 20 metres show closed canopy. Seedlings, sparse young plantations, species and the condition of individual trees are invisible at this scale.

Measure carbon or biodiversity

An extent is an area. Biomass, carbon stock, species richness and ecosystem health need other measurements, most of them on the ground.

Replace the tide table and the boots

Tidal state changes what a sensor sees on a mudflat. Compositing over many dates and a tide sensitivity reduce the effect; they do not remove it. Field truth remains field truth.

What would change this picture

The programme record sets out the work: Independent validation in the field and against very-high-resolution imagery, with external methodology review.

Month 1 would start on a grant agreement, indicatively mid-2027. Nothing in this plan is funded, contracted or under way, and no partner or pilot host has been named.

Think the method is wrong somewhere?

Say where. The method file, the decision steps and the public inputs are open, and a qualified reviewer can be given the withheld files under a short agreement. Criticism that holds is published with the record.
