What a refusal looks like

On 13 October 2026 a measurement of the 2020 mangrove canopy of a 1,937 ha area in Cần Giờ, Viet Nam, finished without an error and declined to give an answer. This note walks through what happened, using only what the public record shows.

What was asked

The claim was narrow on purpose: the extent of mangrove canopy inside one fixed boundary during one dry season, 1 January 2020 to 30 April 2020. The boundary, the thresholds, the tolerances and the rule for deciding were written into a method file, and that file was hashed, before any result was computed.

The evidence came in two groups, optical scenes from Sentinel-2 and radar acquisitions from Sentinel-1, and was compared with an external reference, Global Mangrove Watch. 24 optical scenes were found for the period and 16 passed the admission rules; all 19 radar acquisitions were used. Every request to a data source left a receipt: 296 in all.

Four steps passed

StepRuleWhat the record shows
01Each group covers at least 99.00 % of the AOI and has valid observations for at least 90.00 %Coverage 100.00 % (optical) and 100.00 % (radar); valid fraction 99.50 % (optical) and 100.00 % (radar)
02Both evidence groups and the external reference are presentReference covers 100.00 % of the AOI
03Temporal rules: every acquisition inside the epoch; the reference period contains the epochMet
04Primary decision: groups within 10.00 % of each other, reference within 10.00 % of eachSUPPORTED

At this point most pipelines would stop and report. The primary decision was SUPPORTED: the two sensor groups agreed with each other and with the reference, within the tolerance set in advance.

The fifth step did not

The rule: Robustness: the decision is the same with each threshold moved to either end of its pre-registered bracket.

The record: primary SUPPORTED, mvi_low SUPPORTED, mvi_high CONFLICTING, vh_low SUPPORTED, vh_high CONFLICTING.

So the outcome is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, with the reason code U_THRESHOLD_SENSITIVE, and step six, which would have stated an interval, never ran.

What a threshold bracket is

A satellite does not label pixels “mangrove”. It returns numbers: how green and wet the surface looks in optical bands, how strongly it scatters a radar pulse. A threshold turns each number into a yes or a no.

There is no single correct threshold. Two careful analysts would pick slightly different values and both could defend their choice. The bracket is the range of defensible choices, registered before the data is opened.

If the conclusion is the same everywhere inside the bracket, it belongs to the site. If it flips somewhere inside the bracket, it belongs to the analyst. Here it flipped at the upper end of both brackets.

Why not publish the primary figure with a caveat?

Because figures travel and caveats do not. A number with a footnote in a record becomes a number without one in a slide, a proposal or a headline.

The programme’s rule is therefore blunt. For a refused result no extent figure or range is published, including the values of the individual evidence groups and any map from which they could be read. The files exist and their digests are public, so a reviewer who is given access can confirm they hold the same bytes.

What this does and does not tell you

  • It shows that the refusal works on real data. The mechanism was not staged; it was registered in advance and it fired.
  • It does not show that the method is right. A method can refuse for good reasons and still be poor. No field data and no independent reviewer have tested it yet. The record says “not reviewed”.
  • It says nothing about the mangroves of Cần Giờ, in 2020 or now, and nothing against the reference map.
  • It tells you what “supported” will mean when a BCCS record carries that word: that the answer survived every pre-registered attempt to move it.

What happens next

The next step is an open decision, published with the claim:

  • A. Accept INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE as the C-01 result for the 2020 epoch and keep it unchanged.
  • B. Commission an independent calibration of the MVI and VH thresholds for Can Gio (data not used in this result), pre-register a method d001-c01-0.2 from it, and evaluate it as a new, separately versioned measurement.
  • C. Treat the bracket widths themselves as the open question for external review (Gate 7) before any new version.

Whichever is chosen, the 2020 result is not edited. A new attempt would be a new, separately versioned measurement with its own method file.
