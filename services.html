Services and prices

Fixed scope, a written deliverable, a price before work starts

Three services and one small first step. You pay for the record and the work behind it. The fee does not depend on the answer, and a refusal costs the same as a figure.

One site · one period · one question

Site evidence check

For a funder, a programme or a project that needs an independent, dated answer to one area question.

Fixed price per site and periodStated in the written scope before any work starts. No hourly billing. Run as a pilot.

You send
The boundary of the area as a file, the period, and the claim in one sentence, for example “mangrove canopy covered this area in the dry season of a given year”. Any map you already rely on.
We do
  • Write the method: the claim, the boundary, the thresholds with their brackets, the tolerances and the decision rule. You receive it and its SHA-256 before any data is opened.
  • Acquire Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 radar scenes and an external reference map, with a receipt for every request.
  • Compute offline from the frozen inputs, compare the two sensor groups with each other and with the reference, and repeat the decision at the ends of every bracket.
You receive
  • An evidence record: the outcome (supported with an interval, conflicting, or insufficient evidence) and the steps that led to it.
  • The inventory of inputs with sizes and digests, and what is needed to re-run the computation.
  • A plain-language summary of what the evidence supports, what it does not, and what was not examined.

The record is yours and stays private unless you release it.

Takes
Typically four weeks from an agreed boundary and claim. The written scope gives the date.
Know this first

The method that exists today answers one kind of question: mangrove canopy extent in one period, from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, compared with Global Mangrove Watch. It has been run once in public, where it refused to give a figure. It has not been validated in the field. A run on your site is a pilot and is described as one.

Satellites at this resolution see closed canopy. They do not see newly planted seedlings, species, biomass or carbon.

Before verification, rating or a funding decision

Evidence readiness review

For carbon, nature-asset and restoration projects whose claims will be examined by a verifier, a rating agency, a funder or the press.

USD 9,500Fixed. 50 % on confirmation of scope, 50 % on delivery. The same instrument as KRYONIS commission C-03 (opens kryonis.global).

You send
The claims as you make them, and the material behind them: maps, boundaries, monitoring data, method documents, earlier findings.
We do
  • Set each claim against the evidence supplied for it, and note what is missing.
  • Try to regenerate the headline figures from what you sent: same data, same steps, same result?
  • Check boundaries, dates, versions and provenance. Where the question allows, compare area claims with open satellite sources.
You receive
  • A written review in which every claim is marked supported, not supported, or not checkable from the material.
  • The gaps in order of consequence, with what to fix, what to prove and what to withdraw.
  • A closing exchange in writing with your principals.

Confidential by default. The document belongs to you.

Takes
21 days from receipt of the material.
Know this first
This is a review by an independent specialist, not a verification statement and not an assurance engagement. BCCS is not an accredited validation and verification body and does not replace one.

Your records, kept the way ours are

Evidence room

For a programme, a fund or a research group that must keep its own evidence in a form others can inspect.

By written proposalSet-up and a yearly fee, quoted for your programme after a written exchange about what it must hold.

What it is
A dedicated installation of the software that runs the BCCS Evidence Observatory (opens observatory.bccs.bio), on a server for your programme alone.
It includes
  • A registry of immutable records. A correction is a new record that names the one it replaces.
  • Intake that recomputes every size and SHA-256 and refuses a package that does not match itself.
  • A private workspace with signed-in roles and an audit trail.
  • A publication step: nothing is public until a named person approves the set.
  • A public reading room, and a static copy that can be hosted anywhere.
Know this first

It is one installation per programme, not a self-service product with accounts for many organisations. Record types beyond those of the demonstrator are added as part of the engagement.

The software has been reviewed and tested by its builder. It has not had an independent security audit.

The small first step

One question, answered in writing

For anyone who needs a second opinion on one point before deciding anything larger.

USD 850Fixed. The same instrument as KRYONIS commission C-00 (opens kryonis.global).

You send
One question about a claim, a method or a dataset. “Can this figure be re-run from what the report gives?” “What would a verifier ask about this boundary?” “Is satellite data able to answer this at all?”
You receive
A memo of five to seven pages: the answer, the evidence it rests on, the case against it, and the next decision it opens.
Takes
Five working days from confirmation of scope.

How an engagement runs

  1. You write. A few sentences on the site, the claim and the decision it feeds. No call and no deck are needed.
  2. You receive a written scope within two working days: what will be delivered, by which date, at which price. If the question is outside what the evidence can answer, the reply says so and nothing is charged.
  3. You confirm. KRYONIS Sovereign Systems Limited (Hong Kong SAR) sends the invoice. Work starts on confirmation.
  4. The work is done in writing. Questions and interim findings reach you by email, so there is a record of what was asked and answered.
  5. Delivery. One round of clarification is included.

Terms that do not move

  • The fee never depends on the outcome. Nobody is charged for a favourable conclusion.
  • Who commissioned a record and who paid for it is written in the record.
  • Your material stays private. Nothing is published without your written release.
  • The method is fixed before the data is opened and is not changed afterwards.
  • A record is not a certificate and is never sold as one.

When this is not the right service

  • You need a verification statement for a registry. That takes an accredited validation and verification body. A readiness review can come before it, not instead of it.
  • You need survival counts for a planting that is one or two years old. That is field work. Satellite canopy evidence becomes useful some years after planting.
  • You need carbon stock, biomass or biodiversity. None of these is measured here.
  • You need a favourable answer by a date. The method may refuse, and the fee is the same.

Not sure which side of the line your question falls on? The evidence readiness checklist takes five minutes and asks nothing about who you are.

Have a claim that someone else must be able to check?

Describe the site, the claim and the decision it feeds. You receive a written scope with a deliverable, a date and a price within two working days. If open satellite data cannot answer the question, the reply says so.
